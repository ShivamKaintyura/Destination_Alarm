Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

Destination Alarm is an Android app that rings when you are about to reach a destination you choose. It is built to work without an account and to keep your data on your own device.

Short version: no account, no analytics, no advertising ID, no location history uploaded. Your trips and saved places live only in the app's storage on your phone.

1. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or date of birth.
  • No account, password or sign-in of any kind.
  • No advertising identifier and no advertising SDK.
  • No analytics, crash-tracking or third-party tracker SDK.
  • No continuous location history, and no copy of your trips on any server.

2. Information stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, in Android's private app storage:

  • Your current destination: name, latitude, longitude, radius and travel mode.
  • Saved places you create (for example Home, Work or Gym).
  • Your alarm sound choice, vibration setting and a first-launch flag.

This data is not transmitted to us. Uninstalling the app, or using Settings → Delete all data, removes it immediately.

3. Network requests

To show a map and find places, the app uses free, OpenStreetMap-based services. When you search, the text you type — and, for address lookups, an approximate coordinate — is sent to those services so they can return a result:

  • Photon and Nominatim — place search and reverse geocoding.
  • OSRM — route distance and ETA between your location and the destination.
  • OpenFreeMap — map tiles for the map you see.

These requests are necessary to deliver the feature you asked for. They are not linked to any account or identity, and we do not operate a server that logs them. Each provider may process standard technical data (such as an IP address) under its own policy.

4. Permissions and why they are needed

  • Location (including background) — to measure the distance to your destination while the screen is off.
  • Notifications — for the ongoing trip notification and the full-screen alarm.
  • Vibrate — to buzz when the alarm rings, if you keep vibration on.
  • Audio settings — to raise the alarm volume while ringing and restore it afterwards.
  • Ignore battery optimizations (optional) — so the alarm is not delayed by the system.

Location is used only while a trip is armed, and only to decide when to ring.

5. Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or share personal data. There is no data to sell: the app does not collect any. We do not use your data for advertising or profiling.

6. Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

7. Data deletion

Open Settings → Delete all data inside the app to erase your destination, saved places, sound choice and preferences from the device. Uninstalling the app has the same effect. Because nothing is stored on a server, deletion is immediate and complete.

8. Changes to this policy

If a future version adds an optional feature that needs data (for example optional sync), this policy will be updated before that feature ships, and the change will be described in the app's release notes.

9. Contact

For privacy questions, use Settings → Send feedback in the app, or the contact address listed on the app's store page.
